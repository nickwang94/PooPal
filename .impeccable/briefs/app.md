# Surface brief: PooPal app (all screens)

Mode: Operate. Platform: ios. Native controls, Dynamic Type, light + dark with an in-app switch.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A bathroom-tile world for a gut log: every day is a glazed tile, and the log itself fills tiles. Refuses the category default of pastel cards with a cartoon mascot and a grid of same-size stat tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Porcelain-white ground (deep ink-teal in dark), one committed glaze teal for the shell's action and data, warm amber and coral only for dry/loose Bristol states. Rounded-square tiles with a soft glaze highlight and real offset shadow; hairline grout between grouped rows. SF Pro for all UI, tabular digits; the geometric monoline PooPal logotype is the only display voice.

STORY: Open, see today's count and time since last at a glance, tap the big glaze tile to log, optionally add type and note. History reads as a tile mosaic; trends and stats stay plain and exact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Logotype top-left, settings top-right; large count line and status sentence; a full-width glaze tile button (about a quarter of the screen) with today's small tiles inside; secondary "补记" below; this week's seven tiles.

FORM: Tile mosaic, candidate 1 of 7 (tile mosaic, pharmacy label band, tear-off almanac, WC wayfinding signage, reward sticker chart, train ticket stub, toilet-paper perforation). Seed key: none (concept-seed unavailable offline; direction chosen by hand on the user's delegation).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
