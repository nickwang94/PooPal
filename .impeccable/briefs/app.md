# Surface brief: PooPal app (all screens)

Mode: Operate. Platform: ios. Native controls, Dynamic Type, light + dark with an in-app switch.

Owner feedback that shaped this round: v3 (glazed bathroom tile, teal) read as "too business". This is a personal daily-habit app: warm, friendly, soft, a bit of character and delight, tasteful not childish.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A sticker habit journal (手帐) for your gut: every visit is a round die-cut sticker you press onto the day, and a small round buddy keeps you company. Refuses both the clinical health-dashboard (teal, stat grids) and the cartoon-poop kids' app.

OWN-WORLD: Warm-milk ground (plum-black in dark), sunny marigold as the brand field, plum-cocoa ink and a plum interactive tint; Bristol states in tangerine (dry), leaf (ideal), sky (loose). Round stickers with a white die-cut edge and a soft lift shadow; large soft panels. SF Rounded for everything, monospaced digits; the round-cap PooPal logotype is the only display voice.

STORY: Open, the buddy greets you and says how you are doing; press the big marigold sticker to log, a sticker pops into today; optionally add type and note. The week and month read as a sticker sheet; numbers stay plain and exact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Logotype top-left, settings top-right; buddy beside a warm greeting and "今天 N 次"; a full-width marigold sticker button (about a quarter of the screen) with today's stickers on it; a soft secondary "补一条"; this week's seven round stickers.

FORM: Sticker habit journal, candidate 1 of 7 (sticker journal, probiotic-drink bottle packaging, fruit-cup lids, morning-tea ritual, kids' reward chart, tear-off almanac, bath toys). Seed key: none (concept-seed unavailable offline; direction chosen by hand on the owner's delegation).

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: logging presses a sticker: the button squishes, a new sticker pops onto it with a spring, a few confetti dots burst, the buddy hops. Reduce Motion turns this into a crossfade.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
